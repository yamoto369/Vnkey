How to install

Windows 10 and Windows 11

  1. Download the installer for your architecture (x64 or ARM64), then open it.
  2. Follow the installer. Administrator rights are not required.
  3. Vikey runs in the system tray. Press Alt+Z to switch English/Vietnamese.

If SmartScreen displays a warning, continue only if you downloaded the installer from an official Vikey release source.

Portable builds: x64 (zip) · ARM64 (zip)

SHA-256: WIN_X64_SHA (x64) · WIN_ARM64_SHA (ARM64)
